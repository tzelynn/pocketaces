Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
Singtel-UOB Card
Card upgrade for existing Singtel-UOB Cardmembers (Card issued before 27
August 2025)
1. Why am I receiving a Singtel-UOB Visa Signature Card?
We have upgraded your Singtel-UOB Visa Platinum Card to the Singtel-UOB
Visa Signature Card. As a Singtel-UOB Visa Signature cardmember, you will
enjoy Visa Signature’s suite of benefits and privileges such as exclusive
discounts on dining, travel, shopping and lifestyle activities. For more details, visit
uob.com.sg/singtel.
Please note that you will not be liable for any unauthorized amount charged to
the new card until you have activated it. However, all recurring payments and
other payment instructions will be automatically transferred from your existing
card to your new Singtel-UOB Visa Signature Card, and you will be liable for
such transferred payments. If your existing card expires before you activate your
new card, existing recurring payment instructions will be declined.
2. Is there a change in the card design?
Yes, we are pleased to present you a new card design, along with the new
benefits. Also, you will receive benefits as a Visa Signature customer.
3. When will I receive my new Singtel-UOB Card?
We are gradually replacing your Singtel-UOB Card from August 2025 onwards.
Meanwhile, you can continue to use your existing Singtel-UOB Card and enjoy all
the new Singtel-UOB Card benefits with effect from 27 August 2025. You will
enjoy the Visa Signature benefits upon activating your new Singtel-UOB Visa
Signature Card.
However, if you would like to receive the new Card earlier, please reach out to us
via our 24-hour Customer Service Hotline at 1800 222 2121.
4. Can I use my new Singtel-UOB Visa Signature Card immediately?
For security purposes, your new Singtel-UOB Visa Signature Card must be
activated before you can conduct any transaction. Ways to activate your new
card can be found in the mailer accompanying your new card.
5. What will happen to my existing Singtel-UOB Visa Platinum Card after I
activate the new card?Your existing Singtel-UOB Platinum Card will be deactivated automatically once
your new Singtel-UOB Visa Signature Card is successfully activated. Please
destroy your existing card after activating the new card.
6. Can I continue to use my existing Singtel-UOB Visa Platinum Card until it
expires?
You can use your existing Singtel-UOB Visa Platinum Card until it expires or until
you have activated the new Singtel-UOB Visa Signature Card (whichever is
earlier). However, to avoid potential disruption to your card usage, you are
strongly encouraged to activate and use the new Singtel-UOB Visa Signature
Card.
7. Will there be a change to my existing Singtel-UOB Visa Platinum Card 16-
digit card number and expiry date?
Your new Singtel-UOB Visa Signature Card will continue to use the same 16-digit
card number of your existing Singtel-UOB Visa Platinum Card. However, for
security purposes, your new Singtel-UOB Visa Signature Card will contain a new
expiry date and CVV.
8. Will I receive a new PIN?
No, your current PIN will remain valid on your new Singtel-UOB Visa Signature
Card.
9. If I have set my Singtel-UOB Visa Platinum Card as the default card for
online and/or in-app payments, will it be affected if I start using my new
Singtel-UOB Visa Signature Card?
No, your existing online and/in-app payments will not be affected. However, you
may be prompted to update your expiry date and CVV of your new Singtel-UOB
Visa Signature Card.
10. Will the recurring bill payment arrangements, GIRO instructions and other
account linkages on my existing Singtel-UOB Visa Platinum Card be
affected?
Existing recurring bill payment arrangements, GIRO instructions and other
account linkages on your existing Singtel-UOB Visa Platinum Card will not be
affected. However, you may be prompted to update your expiry date and CVV of
your new Singtel-UOB Visa Signature Card.11. Will there be additional Credit Limit granted to me in view of the newly
issued Singtel-UOB Visa Signature Card?
There will be no change to the Credit Limit granted to you across all Cards
issued to you or for your Card Account in respect of the new Singtel-UOB Visa
Signature Card
12. If I have digitised my Singtel-UOB Visa Platinum Card on my mobile
payment wallet, will it be affected if I start using my new Singtel-UOB Visa
Signature Card?
Prior to activation of your new Singtel-UOB Visa Signature Card, your existing
Singtel-UOB Visa Platinum Card digitized on your mobile payment wallet will not
be affected and will be valid for use until its expiry date. After the activation of
your new Singtel-UOB Visa Signature Card, the card expiry date and CVV details
in your mobile payment wallet will be automatically updated with the new expiry
date and CVV of your new Singtel-UOB Visa Signature Card. If you have not
digitised your Singtel-UOB Visa Platinum Card and wish to do so, please activate
and digitise your new Singtel-UOB Visa Signature Card instead.
13. I have activated my Singtel-UOB Visa Signature Card, however in my
mobile payment wallet it is still displaying the Singtel-UOB Visa Platinum
Card image.
To reflect the Singtel-UOB Visa Signature Card image in your mobile payment
wallet, you will need to remove your existing Singtel-UOB Visa Platinum and re-
digitise your new Singtel-UOB Visa Signature Card from your mobile payment
wallet.
14. I have accumulated spend on the Card to qualify for annual Singtel
voucher, will my spend be reset if I start using my new Singtel-UOB
Signature Visa Card?
No, your spend will not be reset. Your new spends on Singtel-UOB Visa
Signature Card will continue to accumulate towards your previous accumulated
spend on your old Card. Visit uob.com.sg/singtel for the details on the Singtel
voucher benefits.
15. Why is UOB changing the Singtel-UOB Card benefits?We are committed to continuously improve our service to better meet your needs
to enhance your overall banking experience with us. These changes are
designed to provide you with more value, flexibility and convenience.
16. I have my Singtel-UOB Card issued before 27 August 2025. When will I start
enjoying the new enhanced benefits?
If you hold a Singtel-UOB Card issued before 27 August 2025, the revised terms
and conditions of the Singtel-UOB Card will take effect on transactions posted to
your Card on or after 27 August 2025.
