TO AUTHORISE ANOTHER PERSON TO COLLECT ON YOUR BEHALF
If you are unable to redeem the Gift personally, please print and complete the authorisation slip set out
in Annex 1 below to authorise another person to redeem the Gift on your behalf.
To redeem the Gift, the authorised person must present all of the following at the time of redemption:
• A forwarded copy of the Redemption SMS on his/her mobile phone
• A hardcopy of the original duly signed authorisation slip in the form set out in Annex 1 below
• A copy of your NRIC/Passport for identification purposes at the redemption centre
• The authorised person’s original NRIC/Passport for identification purposes at the redemption centre
In the event that any of the above documents are not presented at the time of redemption, the authorised
person will not be entitled to redeem the Gift.
