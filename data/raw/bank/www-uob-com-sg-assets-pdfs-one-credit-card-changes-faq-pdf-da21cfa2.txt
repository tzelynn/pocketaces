Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) for existing UOB One Credit Cardmembers
(cards issued before 1 July 2025)
1. I am an existing UOB One Cardmember, do I need to register/apply to enjoy the new additional
cashback benefits on my grocery spend?
For existing UOB One Credit Cardmembers issued before 1 July 2025, you will automatically enjoy the
following added benefits from 1 July 2025:
(i) Monthly cashback cap for additional cashback is increased from S$100 to S$120
The total cashback you can earn in a year is increased from S$2,000 to S$2,240; and
(ii) New additional cashback of up to 4.67% on your Grocery transactions (with MCC 5411) if you
have met a minimum of $1,000 or $2,000 monthly spend for your qualifying spend quarter.
Please note that minimum spend and other card conditions apply. Please note that 7-Eleven, Guardian
and UOB Travel transactions will no longer earn additional cashback with effect from 1 August 2025.

## RESTRICTED ##3. Will the One Credit Card revision effective from 1 July 2025 affect the qualifying minimum card
spend for bonus interest on my One Account balance?
No, the One Credit Card revision effective from 1 July 2025 will not affect the minimum card spend required
to earn bonus interest on your One Account balance. The minimum card spend on your One Credit Card to
earn bonus interest on your One Account balance will remain at S$500 from 1 July 2025. Please refer to
One Credit Card page for details on the revisions to the One Credit Card effective 1 July 2025.
