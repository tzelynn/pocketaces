1. OCBC FRANK Credit Card Programme
(a) The OCBC FRANK Credit Card (“Card”) is a card bearing the name VISA and/or the service
mark of VISA issued by Oversea-Chinese Banking Corporation Limited (“OCBC Bank”) which
comes with the following features:
(i) 8% Cashback on Foreign Currency Transactions (refer to Clause 3(a)), capped at
S$25
(ii) 8% Cashback on SGD Transactions made Online and via Mobile Contactless (refer
to Clause 3(b)), capped at S$25
(iii) Extra 2% Cashback on transactions made at selected green merchants (refer to
Clause 4(a)), capped at S$25
(iv) 0.3% Cashback on all other eligible spend, capped at S$25
(each and collectively, “Cashback”).
(b) The maximum Cashback that one Card Account (from transactions posted by both the Principal
and all Supplementary Cardmembers) can earn in any calendar month is S$100.
(c) To be eligible for the above Cashback, Cardmembers must spend a minimum of S$800 based
on eligible transactions posted in the same calendar month on the OCBC FRANK Credit Card
(the “Minimum Spend Requirement”). Transactions set out in Clause 2 below are excluded.
Principal and Supplementary Cardmembers’ spending are consolidated under the Card
Account.
(d) If the Minimum Spend Requirement is not met in the same calendar month, Cardmembers will
earn 0.3% Cashback on all eligible spend.
