Promotion
1. The Platinum Card Sign Up Promotion (the “Promotion”) is valid from 1 September 2026 to 31 January
2027, both dates inclusive (the “Promotion Period”).
2. To be eligible for this Promotion, you must satisfy all the following criteria:
2.1 You do not hold an existing basic or supplementary consumer Card from American Express.
2.2 You do not previously hold a basic or supplementary consumer Card from American Express that
was terminated or otherwise cancelled in the last twenty-four (24) months prior to the application.
2.3 You must apply for The Platinum Card with American Express within the Promotion Period and
receive approval within forty-five (45) days from the date of application.
2.4 You must pay the full Annual Membership Fee of S$1,744 (incl. of 9% GST) in the first monthly
statement.
2.5 You must not have already submitted an application for a basic Platinum Card under any other
promotion(s).
