Promotion
1. The American Express Singapore Airlines KrisFlyer Ascend Credit Card Sign Up Promotion (the
“Promotion”) is valid from 17 June 2026 to 31 January 2027, both dates inclusive (the “Promotion Period”).
2. To be eligible for this Promotion, you must satisfy all the following criteria:
2.1 You do not hold an existing basic or supplementary consumer Card from American Express.
2.2 You do not previously hold a basic or supplementary consumer Card from American Express that
was terminated or otherwise cancelled in the last twenty-four (24) months prior to the application.
2.3 You must apply for the Singapore Airlines KrisFlyer Ascend Credit Card with American Express
within the Promotion Period and receive approval within forty-five (45) days from the date of
application.
2.4 You must pay the full Annual Membership Fee of S$397.85 (incl. of 9% GST) in the first monthly
statement.
2.5 You must not have already submitted an application for a basic American Express Singapore
Airlines KrisFlyer Ascend Credit Card under any other promotion(s).

more information.
