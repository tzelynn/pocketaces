3. Lazada Gift Card
3.1. For each calendar month which a Principal Cardmember fulfils a Minimum Spend of S$500, the Principal
Cardmember will be eligible to receive rebates in the form of Lazada Gift Card on the Principal Cardmember’s
eligible spend for that month in the following manner based on transaction category:
Rebates earned per transaction category Cap on rebates value
20% rebates on Lazada Spend S$20
6% rebates on RedMart Spend S$20
5% rebates on Dining, Entertainment and Transport Spend S$25
0.3% rebates on all other spend S$25
The Minimum Spend requirement, rebates and cap on rebates value reflected in this clause apply from 1
September 2024 to 31 January 2027 (both dates inclusive).
For example:
If a Principal Cardmember fulfils a Minimum Spend of S$500 for a particular month and the Card Transactions
during that month comprise of Lazada Spend, RedMart Spend, Dining, Entertainment and Transport Spend
and other spend, the Principal Cardmember will receive the following:
• Rebates in the form of Lazada Gift Card with a value corresponding to 20% of the Lazada Spend
amount successfully charged to the Principal Cardmember’s Card Account and posted on UOB’s
system during that month;
• Rebates in the form of Lazada Gift Card with a value corresponding to 6% of the RedMart Spend
amount successfully charged to the Principal Cardmember’s Card Account and posted on UOB’s
system during that month;
• Rebates in the form of Lazada Gift Card with a value corresponding to 5% of the Dining,
Entertainment and Transport Spend amount successfully charged to the Principal Cardmember’s
Card Account and posted on UOB’s system during that month; and

will be included in the computation of Principal Cardmember’s Minimum Spend and Lazada Gift Card to be

Cardmember’s Card is added to will be deemed as the Principal Cardmember’s Lazada Account. If a Principal

file in the Lazada Wallet of a Lazada Account due to omission or whatsoever reasons, any Lazada Gift Card

3.6. If a Principal Cardmember is eligible to receive the Lazada Gift Card, the Lazada Gift Card will be credited to
